<h2>Privacy Policy — Colorist Tool Kit Lite</h2>

**Last updated:** August 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Colorist Tool Kit Lite** (“the App”) handles information.

<h3>Information you provide in the App</h3>

The App includes tools such as checklists, text conversion, and calculators. Any information you enter (for example notes, checklist items, or text) is stored <strong>locally on your device</strong>. The App does not require an account.

<h3>Data collection</h3>

The App does <strong>not</strong> include third‑party analytics or advertising SDKs.

The App may still handle limited technical information as part of normal operation (for example device and system information used by iOS), but the App is designed to function without collecting user analytics.

<h3>Web content and third‑party services</h3>

The App may display web content from third‑party services, including:

<ul>
<li>YouTube (embedded web content)</li>
<li>blake‑jones.com pages (Training Services, OFX Plugin Webshop)</li>
</ul>

When you view embedded web content, those services may collect information according to their own privacy policies. Their collection and use of data is governed by their policies, not this App.

<ul>
<li><a href="https://policies.google.com/privacy" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">YouTube / Google Privacy Policy</a></li>
</ul>

<h3>Sharing</h3>

If you use iOS sharing features (for example Share Sheet) to export text, the content you choose to share will be sent using the destination app/service you select.

<h3>Children</h3>

The App is not intended for use by children under 13.
