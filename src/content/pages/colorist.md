Blake started as a colorist in Hollywood and graded such films as “2001: A Space Odyssey” and “Poltergeist” to name only a few of the numerous titles he has worked on. Recently Blake was the digital intermediate colorist for Federico Zampaglione’s film “Shadow”. Most recently Blake was senior colorist for the film “Monsoon Empire”. Blake also just completed a re-edit and color grading for the documentary "Romanov".
